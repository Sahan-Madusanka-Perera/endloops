## Platform

web

## Stack

React (Vite) + Tailwind CSS + React Router. The brief names React and Tailwind as the company's day-to-day stack. Deploy target: a static host (Vercel-ready). Local git only; the owner pushes and deploys under their own accounts.

## Users

Visitors are finance professionals deciding whether Endloops is worth a conversation: accountants, auditors, in-house finance teams and property management companies, mostly in Dubai and the wider GCC. Their work is detailed, deadline-bound and audited; they are skeptical of tools they cannot trace or trust. A secondary audience is the hiring panel reviewing this build as an intern assignment (UI/UX and Product Design).

## Product Purpose

Endloops builds automation for financial work. It began as the technology department of Alkazim, an accounting and audit firm in Dubai. The software prepares the repetitive work (reading documents, matching, checking against rules, drafting schedules and working papers) and the professional reviews and decides. Success for the site: within a few seconds a visitor understands what Endloops does, who it is for and why it matters, can tell the two product types apart, and takes the call to action (talk to us / request a demo).

## Positioning

Built inside a working audit firm, by people who do the work, so the automation follows how audits and finance reviews actually run. The principle no neighbor can copy honestly: the software prepares, the professional signs off. Sign-off always stays with people.

## Operating Context

Invoices, purchase orders, contracts, bank and supplier statements, ledgers, quotes and tenders, budgets, working papers, schedules, lead sheets, audit files, review notes, sign-off. Year-end close and audit fieldwork deadlines. Property management: service-charge budgets, escrow accounts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Two product types, which the site must show and distinguish:

1. End-to-end solutions: run a whole process or financial service start to finish. Budget review; escrow audit; financial audit; interim audit; budget preparation for property management companies.
2. Standalone tools: focused, usable on their own. Purchase-order extraction; vouching automation; expense working automation; financial report review; tender verification; reconciliation.

Some products are in use and others are in development; which is which is not confirmed, so the site must not label individual products as live or upcoming. The range will keep growing.

Technology capabilities: read documents (extract figures and details from invoices, POs, contracts, statements); compare and match (documents vs ledgers, quotes vs each other); check against rules (missing items, mismatched totals, duplicates, out-of-limit items); prepare drafts (schedules, working papers, reports ready for review); handle bulk work (hundreds of documents at once).

Ambition: become the trusted automation partner for accounting, audit and property finance teams in the GCC and beyond.

## Brand Commitments

- Premium: refined, confident, high quality; firms trust it with financial data.
- Professional and approachable; creativity welcome where it adds polish and clarity.
- Colours: white, green, blue and purple, with dark navy for text. The logo's green and teal anchor the palette; extending it is allowed.
- Logo: use as supplied. No redrawing, recolouring or stretching. Current file is the JPEG embedded in the brief (439x127, white ground), at `public/endloops-logo.jpg`; keep it on white or near-white grounds. Replace with the original file if one is supplied.
- The brief and logo are confidential, used only for this assignment.

## Evidence on Hand

- The brief's own facts (above). No customer names, testimonials, case studies, metrics, pricing, certifications or press exist. Do not invent them.
- Demonstration data (sample invoices, ledger lines, flags) may be authored for product demos and must be labelled as sample data.
- Contact details are not supplied; the contact form is front-end only and any address shown is a marked placeholder.

## Product Principles

1. The professional decides. Every surface shows automation handing work to a person, never replacing their judgement.
2. Traceable, not magic. Show where every figure and flag came from.
3. Clarity over spectacle. Finance users read precisely; nothing decorative may obscure a fact.
4. Two shapes of product, one engine. End-to-end solutions and standalone tools share the same capabilities and differ in scope.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, alt text, reduced-motion support, semantic structure. Region is bilingual (English/Arabic); the site ships in English, with layout that would not break under RTL later.
